# A talk

It is heard once, so write for the ear: short sentences, one idea each, plain words, no references to "the figure above". Numbers are rounded and said the way a person says them.

Ask how long it runs if nobody said. Plan on about 130 English words or 220 Chinese characters a minute, and give each section its minutes in the heading.

Start with the problem as the audience feels it, tell how the work went, including what failed, since that is what people remember, and end on one sentence they can repeat. Sections are the spoken parts, written out in full as they will be said.
