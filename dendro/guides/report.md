# A project report

Someone who was not there reads it once and knows where the project stands. Most of them stop after the first paragraph, so that paragraph carries the conclusion.

A shape to adjust, not fill in:

- Where it stands: goal, stage, the main result so far.
- What was done: the few steps that moved it, in order, each with why it was done.
- Decisions: what was chosen, over what, and why.
- Problems: what is open, what was solved and how.
- Next: only what the records say is next.

A person reading the first lines of each section should follow the whole story. Prefer one table or list of results to a paragraph of numbers.
