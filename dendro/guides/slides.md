# A slide deck

Sections are slides, and each heading is the conclusion of its slide, a full short sentence: "The cache cut load time by half", not "Results". Read in order, the headings alone tell the story.

One idea a slide and a few words on it: the text field holds what the slide shows and the speaker's words go in the notes of the file. Show a figure, a table or a number rather than a paragraph.

Allow about a minute and a half a slide. Open with why this matters to this audience, not with an outline; end on what the audience should remember or decide. Keep open problems in: a deck that hides them loses the room at the first question.

To make a file, use a slide skill or library if you have one, then pass its path as `file` to dendro_output. Without one, give the outline and say so.
