# The thread of the work

For the people doing the work, to see their own thinking again. Not a chronicle: the question the project was really asking, and how the answer changed.

Follow the turns: what was believed, what changed it, what was tried and dropped and why. Sections are the turns, each heading the shift in a sentence: "From speed to memory: the search was never the bottleneck".

Corrections, dropped ideas and reversed decisions matter most here. End with what is settled, what is still open, and any contradiction between them or between what was said and what was done. Point it out plainly, even if nobody asked.
